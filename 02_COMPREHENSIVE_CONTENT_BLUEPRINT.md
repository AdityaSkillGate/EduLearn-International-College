## 3. Academic Programs & Curriculum Specifications (All 21 Programs)

### 3.1 Diploma Programs (13 Programs)

#### Common Framework for Technical & Business Diplomas:
- **Standard Duration:** 6 Months (Full-Time: 5 days/wk x 3 hrs/day; Part-Time: 1 day/wk x 4 hrs/day).
- **Total Contact Hours:** 96 Contact Hours (6 Modules x 16 Hours per module).
- **Delivery Mode:** Face-to-Face (Classroom) or E-Learning (where designated).
- **Minimum Attendance:** Mandatory 80% attendance requirement.
- **Teacher-Student Ratio:** Lecture 1:14.
- **Standard Assessment Breakdown:** Written Exam (40%) + Practical Exam / Assignment (60%).
- **Standard Entry Requirements:**
  - Minimum 3 GCE 'O' Level passes including English at C6; **OR**
  - International Secondary Qualification pass (Year 10 equivalent) in 3 subjects including English; **OR**
  - Mature applicants (minimum 25 years old with 3 years of work experience) and a pass in the EIC Entry Proficiency Test.
- **Standard Course Fees:**
  - **Full-Time:** Course Fee: $2,700 ($500 x 6 installments) + Registration Fee: $300 (Non-refundable) = **$3,000 Total**
  - **Part-Time:** Course Fee: $2,400 ($400 x 6 installments) + Registration Fee: $300 (Non-refundable) = **$2,700 Total**

## 4. Singapore Regulatory, Contractual & Financial Governance

### 4.1 CPE Standard PEI-Student Contract (Version 4.1)
EIC executes the mandatory Singapore Committee for Private Education standard contract containing:
- **Schedule A:** Course Details, Permitted Course Duration, Developer/Proprietor, Entry Requirements, Examination Periods.
- **Schedule B:** Course Fees Breakdown (Tuition Fee, Examination Fee, Medical Insurance, Course Materials, Student Pass Fee, Registration Fee, Installment schedule).
- **Schedule C:** Miscellaneous Non-Compulsory Fees.
- **Schedule D:** Refund Policy.
- **Cooling-Off Period:** Clause 3.8 grants a **7-working-day cooling-off period** from contract signing during which 100% of fees paid are refunded upon written notice of termination.

### 4.2 Fee Refund Policy Matrix

| Written Notice Received by College | Refund Percentage (Schedules B & C) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **> 30 days before Course Commencement Date** | **100%** ("Maximum Refund") |
| **Within 30 days before Course Commencement Date** | **75%** |
| **1 to 5 days after Course Commencement Date** | **25%** |
| **6 to 10 days after Course Commencement Date** | **10%** |
| **> 10 days after Course Commencement Date** | **0%** |
| *During 7-day Cooling-Off Period* | **100%** (Minus any consumed fees) |
| *Registration Fee* | **Non-refundable and non-transferable** |

### 4.3 Student Protections & Dispute Resolution
- **Fee Protection:** Required under SSG/CPE regulations for international and private students.
- **Dispute Resolution Mechanism:** Standard 3-stage process: Internal Resolution (College Management) $\rightarrow$ Mediation via Singapore Mediation Centre (SMC) $\rightarrow$ Arbitration via Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC).
